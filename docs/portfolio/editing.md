title: Copy Editing

> **Context**: Interior design company updating [their website](https://www.iqondesign.com/).

> **Purpose**: Review descriptions written by non-native English speaker and propose grammatical and style improvements.

## Urban Loft
<div class="grid cards" markdown>
-   ### ❌ Original Text
	In this apartment we show a simplicity in a form of function, this is an upscale urban feel with a great value. 
	
	We have chosen light colors to brighten the unit and thus it was a great result with which the client was extremely happy.
	
-   ### ✅ Edited Text
	This apartment is a showcase of simplicity in form and function. 
	
	For an upscale urban feel, we chose light colors to brighten up the unit. 
</div>
## Contemporary Practical
<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   ### ❌ Original Text
	This is a contemporary interior design we have created in 2011 in Lithuania for a young couple in the beginning of their life together.

	The combination of colors brings calmness and cozy atmosphere to help relax after a long day in the office or simply to release the stress of the week and enjoy the tranquility of a modern home, easy to maintain and the brightness lifts up the mood.
	
-   ### ✅ Edited Text
	This is a contemporary interior design we created in 2011 in Lithuania for a young couple starting their life together. 
	
	The combination of colors creates a calm and cozy atmosphere to help relax after a long day and enjoy the tranquility of a modern home. 
</div>

## Coastal Tetralogy 1
<div class="grid cards" markdown>

	This first unit we designed in a combination of loft and rustic style. We chose neuter tones and focused mainly on the functionality of the unit. 
	
	As we all know we love the apartment we rent for a holiday to be practical, tastefully decorated and to have everything on a one hand distance.
	
-   ### ✅ Edited Text
	This first unit we designed in a combination of loft and rustic style. We chose neutral tones and focused mainly on the functionality of the unit. 
	
	As we all know, we want the apartment we rent for a holiday to be practical, tastefully decorated and to have everything on hand.

	Different size and colors pillows brig up the mood and helps us here the laughter of girls walking around planning their days during the holiday.
